It is usually not the right range for “what next month’s actual headcount will be.” “We are 95% confident the average headcount trend is between 2,500 and 2,600.” Prediction interval A range for the actual future value. This is the correct concept for your forecast CSV. Apr 2026 forecast = 2554, 80% prediction interval = 2272 to 2836. Lower bound The bottom end of the prediction interval. FORECAST LOWER 80 = 2272 means the low end of the likely range for Apr 2026. Upper bound The top end of the prediction interval. FORECAST UPPER 80 = 2836 means the high end of the likely range for Apr 2026. Point forecast The single best estimate from the model. Apr 2026 = 2554. Confidence level / coverage level The percentage attached to the interval, such as 80%. Higher means a safer but wider range. 80% means the interval is designed to contain the actual value about 8 out of 10 times over many similar forecasts. Forecast error The difference between actual and forecast. If actual is 2615 and forecast is 2617, error is -2 if you use Actual - Forecast. Forecast bias Whether the model tends to over-forecast or under-forecast. Forecast bias = Forecast - Actual; if forecast is 2617 and actual is 2615, bias is +2, so the model over-forecasted. MAE Average absolute miss in actual units. Easy for business users to read. MAE = 47.31 means the forecast missed by about 47 employees per month on average. MAPE Average absolute miss as a percentage of actuals. MAPE = 1.71% means the average miss was about 1.71%.
