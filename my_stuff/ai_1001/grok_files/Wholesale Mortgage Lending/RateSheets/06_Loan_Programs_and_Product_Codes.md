# 06 — Loan Programs & Product Codes

**Prerequisite:** 05 — Base Pricing & Par Rate

**Goal:** Recognize every program block a rate sheet may include and know what loans qualify for each. Each program has its own grid and adjusters.

## Concepts covered
- Conforming vs Government vs Jumbo vs Non-QM
- Conforming/High-Balance/Super-Conforming limits
- Common product codes and term/structure abbreviations
- Why a single rate sheet may contain 20+ product blocks

## Jargon

| # | Term | Definition |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | Conforming Loan | Loan that meets Fannie/Freddie agency guidelines and conforming loan limit. |
| 2 | Conforming Loan Limit (CLL) | Annual FHFA-set cap on conforming loan size (county-specific). |
| 3 | High Balance / Super Conforming | Loans above CLL but at or below high-cost area ceiling. |
| 4 | Jumbo | Loan exceeding the conforming/high-balance limit; priced via investor sheets, not agency. |
| 5 | Non-Conforming | Any loan not meeting agency guidelines. |
| 6 | Non-QM | Loans outside the CFPB Qualified Mortgage definition (Bank Statement, DSCR, Asset Depletion, ITIN, etc.). |
| 7 | Agency Loan | Conforming loan eligible for sale to Fannie or Freddie. |
| 8 | Government Loan / Govie | FHA, VA, or USDA loan. |
| 9 | FHA | Federal Housing Administration-insured loan. |
| 10 | VA | Veterans Affairs-guaranteed loan. |
| 11 | USDA / RD | Rural Development zero-down program. |
| 12 | Ginnie Mae / GNMA | Investor pool for FHA/VA/USDA loans. |
| 13 | HFA Loan | State Housing Finance Agency program (often with DPA). |
| 14 | DPA (Down Payment Assistance) | Second-lien/grant program layered on a first lien. |
| 15 | HomeReady / Home Possible | Fannie/Freddie low-down, low-income programs. |
| 16 | HomeStyle / CHOICERenovation | Renovation loan programs. |
| 17 | Construction-to-Perm (CTP) | Single-close construction + permanent financing. |
| 18 | ARM (3/6, 5/6, 7/6, 10/6 SOFR) | Hybrid adjustable products with initial fixed period then 6-month adjustment. |
| 19 | Interest-Only (IO) | Product with an IO period before amortization begins. |
| 20 | DSCR Loan | Debt-Service-Coverage-Ratio loan for investment properties (no personal income docs). |
| 21 | Bank Statement Loan | Non-QM income calculated from bank deposits. |
| 22 | Product Code / Plan Code | Internal SKU identifying a specific program/term/structure (e.g., "CF30", "FHA30", "JF7/6"). |
| 23 | Term | Loan length: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40 years. |
