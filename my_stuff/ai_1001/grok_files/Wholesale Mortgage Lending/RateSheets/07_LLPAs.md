# 07 — Loan-Level Price Adjustments (LLPAs)

**Prerequisite:** 06 — Loan Programs & Product Codes

**Goal:** Master the agency-defined risk-based pricing adjustments. LLPAs are the single biggest driver of why two borrowers get different prices at the same rate.

## Concepts covered
- The agency LLPA matrices (Fannie & Freddie) and how they pass through to broker rate sheets
- The FICO × LTV grid: the dominant adjuster
- Purpose, occupancy, property type, and product overlays
- The G-Fee and how it influences pricing
- The 2023 LLPA redesign and DTI-based adjusters

## Jargon

| # | Term | Definition |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | LLPA (Loan-Level Price Adjustment) | Agency-mandated price hit (or credit) tied to a loan attribute. |
| 2 | LLPA Matrix | Published grid (Fannie Form/Exhibit, Freddie Exhibit 19) listing every adjuster. |
| 3 | Risk-Based Pricing | Pricing that varies with credit risk attributes. |
| 4 | FICO/LTV Grid | Primary LLPA grid intersecting credit score buckets with LTV bands. |
| 5 | Credit Score Bucket | Range (e.g., 740–759) used to slot a borrower for pricing. |
| 6 | LTV Band | Range (e.g., 70.01–75%) used to slot a loan for pricing. |
| 7 | Cash-Out Refi Adjuster | Additional LLPA applied to cash-out transactions. |
| 8 | Investment Property Adjuster | Hit for non-owner-occupied loans. |
| 9 | Second Home Adjuster | Hit for second-home occupancy (often the same or smaller than NOO). |
| 10 | Condo Adjuster | Hit for condo property type at certain LTVs. |
| 11 | 2–4 Unit Adjuster | Hit for multi-unit properties. |
| 12 | Manufactured Home Adjuster | Hit for MH collateral. |
| 13 | Subordinate Financing Adjuster | Hit when a 2nd lien is present (CLTV-driven). |
| 14 | High-Balance Adjuster | Hit for super-conforming loan amounts. |
| 15 | HPML / HPCT Adjuster | Pricing flag for Higher-Priced Mortgage Loans. |
| 16 | DTI > 40 Adjuster | DTI-based LLPA introduced in the 2023 LLPA changes (later withdrawn but referenced). |
| 17 | G-Fee (Guarantee Fee) | Fee paid to Fannie/Freddie for the credit guarantee; often expressed as ongoing bps but capitalized into LLPAs. |
| 18 | Adverse Market Refinance Fee | 50 bp refi LLPA (active 2020–2021; sometimes referenced historically). |
| 19 | Cumulative Cap | Maximum combined LLPA for a single loan (program-specific). |
| 20 | Waiver / LLPA Waiver | Targeted LLPA elimination for specific affordable products (HomeReady, Home Possible borrowers under AMI). |
| 21 | Special-Feature Code (SFC) | Fannie code that triggers or waives certain LLPAs at delivery. |
| 22 | PMI Adjuster | Investor-pricing add-on tied to MI coverage level. |
