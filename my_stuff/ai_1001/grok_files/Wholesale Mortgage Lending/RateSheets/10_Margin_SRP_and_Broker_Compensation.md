# 10 — Margin, SRP & Broker Compensation

**Prerequisite:** 09 — Rate Locks & Lock Periods

**Goal:** Understand how the broker actually gets paid out of the rate sheet's price — the difference between Borrower-Paid and Lender-Paid Comp, comp plans, and the role of SRP in pricing.

## Concepts covered
- Borrower-Paid Comp (BPC) vs Lender-Paid Comp (LPC)
- LO Comp Rule (Reg Z) and what it forbids
- How comp plans are baked into the rate sheet
- SRP and how it influences the base price the broker sees
- The economics behind why two lenders' sheets look different

## Jargon

| # | Term | Definition |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | Broker Compensation | Money paid to the broker for originating the loan. |
| 2 | Lender-Paid Comp (LPC) | Lender pays broker out of the loan's premium pricing; cost passed through in the rate. |
| 3 | Borrower-Paid Comp (BPC) | Borrower pays broker directly at closing; rate sheet shows pricing net of comp. |
| 4 | Comp Plan | Broker's set comp percentage (e.g., 1.50% – 2.75% of loan amount), filed with the lender. |
| 5 | Comp Plan Change Date | The date a broker may change its LPC plan (typically monthly/quarterly per lender policy). |
| 6 | LO Comp Rule (Reg Z §1026.36) | Rule prohibiting loan-term-based comp; bars dual comp. |
| 7 | Dual Compensation Prohibition | Broker may not be paid by both borrower and lender on the same loan. |
| 8 | Min/Max Comp | Floor/ceiling on a comp plan (e.g., $X min and $Y max per loan). |
| 9 | SRP (Servicing-Released Premium) | Premium an investor pays for servicing rights; usually retained by the lender but factored into the price they publish. |
| 10 | Servicing Retained Pricing | Alternative pricing track where servicing stays with the lender. |
| 11 | Concession to Broker | Lender-funded credit to cover an issue or close pricing gap (lender discretion). |
| 12 | Premium Recapture / Comp Cap | Cap on how much rebate can flow to broker before lender keeps the excess. |
| 13 | Net Price to Broker | Price after lender's margin and SRP are factored in. |
| 14 | Tolerance Cure | Lender credit funded to cure a TRID tolerance violation. |
| 15 | Originator Comp Disclosure | Required disclosure of LPC structure to consumer/regulator. |
| 16 | Anti-Steering Safe Harbor | Broker's requirement to present a range of loan options. |
| 17 | YSP (historical) | Yield Spread Premium; pre-2011 term replaced by LPC/BPC framework. |
