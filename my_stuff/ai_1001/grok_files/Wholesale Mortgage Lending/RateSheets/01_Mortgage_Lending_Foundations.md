# 01 — Mortgage Lending Foundations

**Prerequisite:** None. Start here.

**Goal:** Build the baseline vocabulary every rate sheet assumes you already know. Without these terms, nothing on a rate sheet makes sense.

## Concepts covered
- What a mortgage is and the parties to a loan
- The difference between principal, interest, and APR
- How loan term, amortization, and payment structures work
- The basic risk metrics underwriters and pricing teams care about

## Jargon

| # | Term | Definition |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | Mortgage | A loan secured by real property. |
| 2 | Borrower / Mortgagor | The party receiving the loan and granting the security interest. |
| 3 | Lender / Mortgagee | The party making the loan and holding the lien. |
| 4 | Principal | The original loan amount, or current unpaid balance. |
| 5 | Interest Rate (Note Rate) | The annual percentage charged on the principal balance. |
| 6 | APR (Annual Percentage Rate) | Note rate plus financing-related fees, expressed annually. |
| 7 | Amortization | Gradual paydown of principal over the life of the loan. |
| 8 | Term | Length of the loan, typically 10, 15, 20, or 30 years. |
| 9 | Fixed-Rate Mortgage (FRM) | Rate stays constant for the life of the loan. |
| 10 | Adjustable-Rate Mortgage (ARM) | Rate changes per a defined index/margin after an initial fixed period. |
| 11 | P&I (Principal & Interest) | The portion of the monthly payment that pays the loan itself. |
| 12 | PITI | Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance (the full housing payment). |
| 13 | Escrow / Impound Account | Lender-held account for property taxes and insurance. |
| 14 | LTV (Loan-to-Value) | Loan amount ÷ property value. |
| 15 | CLTV (Combined LTV) | All liens on the property ÷ property value. |
| 16 | DTI (Debt-to-Income) | Monthly debts ÷ gross monthly income. |
| 17 | FICO / Credit Score | Risk score, 300–850, used for pricing and eligibility. |
| 18 | Loan Amount | Dollar amount being financed. |
| 19 | Purchase / Refinance / Cash-Out Refi | The three primary loan purposes. |
| 20 | Owner-Occupied / Second Home / Investment | Occupancy types, each priced differently. |
| 21 | Property Type | SFR, condo, PUD, 2–4 unit, manufactured. |
| 22 | Lien Position | First lien vs second lien (priority of claim). |
| 23 | Underwriting | The credit, income, asset, and collateral review. |
| 24 | Origination | Creation of a new loan. |
| 25 | Servicing | Ongoing payment collection and account management after closing. |
