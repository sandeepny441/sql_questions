# 02 — Wholesale Channel Basics

**Prerequisite:** 01 — Mortgage Lending Foundations

**Goal:** Understand the wholesale origination channel — who issues rate sheets, who consumes them, and how the loan moves from broker to investor. Rate sheets only make sense in the context of these relationships.

## Concepts covered
- The three origination channels: retail, wholesale, correspondent
- The broker → wholesale lender → investor pipeline
- Where the wholesale lender (often called the "wholesaler" or "lender") sits in the value chain
- Who publishes a rate sheet and to whom

## Jargon

| # | Term | Definition |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | Wholesale Lending | A channel where independent mortgage brokers submit loans to a wholesale lender, who underwrites, funds, and typically sells the loan. |
| 2 | Retail Channel | Lender originates directly to the consumer. |
| 3 | Correspondent Channel | A smaller lender underwrites and funds in its own name, then sells the closed loan to a larger investor. |
| 4 | TPO (Third-Party Originator) | Umbrella term for non-retail originators (brokers and mini-correspondents). |
| 5 | Mortgage Broker | Licensed entity/individual who matches borrowers with wholesale lenders. |
| 6 | Wholesale Lender / Wholesaler | The lender that publishes the rate sheet, underwrites, and funds loans submitted by brokers. |
| 7 | Account Executive (AE) | Wholesale lender's salesperson who manages broker relationships. |
| 8 | Aggregator | A large investor that buys closed loans from correspondents. |
| 9 | Investor | End buyer of the loan (Fannie, Freddie, Ginnie pools, banks, private funds). |
| 10 | Sponsor / Sponsoring Lender | The wholesale lender backing a broker-originated loan to the agencies. |
| 11 | Broker Agreement | Contract between broker and wholesale lender defining terms, comp, and reps. |
| 12 | Loan File / Submission | The package the broker sends the wholesaler for underwriting. |
| 13 | Funding | Wire of loan proceeds at closing by the wholesale lender. |
| 14 | Table Funding | Wholesale lender funds in its own name at the closing table. |
| 15 | Warehouse Line | Short-term credit facility correspondents use to fund loans before selling them. |
| 16 | Pull-Through | Percentage of locked loans that actually close and fund. |
| 17 | Fallout | Locked loans that do not close (the opposite of pull-through). |
| 18 | Best Efforts | Loan committed for sale only if it closes. |
| 19 | Mandatory Delivery | Loan committed for sale regardless of closing — penalty if it doesn't fund. |
| 20 | NMLS | Nationwide Multistate Licensing System; all originators and brokers are registered here. |
| 21 | Wholesale Submission Portal | The lender's web platform where brokers register loans, lock rates, and upload docs. |
