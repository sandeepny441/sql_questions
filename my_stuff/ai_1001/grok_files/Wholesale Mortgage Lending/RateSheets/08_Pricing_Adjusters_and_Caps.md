# 08 — Pricing Adjusters & Caps (Beyond LLPAs)

**Prerequisite:** 07 — Loan-Level Price Adjustments (LLPAs)

**Goal:** Understand the *lender-specific* adjusters layered on top of agency LLPAs — state hits, escrow waivers, lender overlays, and pricing caps.

## Concepts covered
- The difference between agency LLPAs and lender adjusters
- State and geographic adjusters
- Escrow waivers and property-tax-only options
- Lender overlays and how they show up on the sheet
- Caps and floors on the all-in price

## Jargon

| # | Term | Definition |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | Lender Adjuster / Lender Overlay | Lender-specific price hit/credit beyond agency LLPAs. |
| 2 | State Adjuster | Hit or credit tied to property state (NY, TX, FL are common examples). |
| 3 | County Adjuster | Geographic hit for high-cost or high-risk counties. |
| 4 | Escrow Waiver Adjuster | Pricing hit when borrower waives escrow for taxes/insurance. |
| 5 | Tax-Only / Insurance-Only Waiver | Partial escrow waiver pricing. |
| 6 | MI Type Adjuster | Hit/credit based on BPMI vs LPMI vs Split MI. |
| 7 | LPMI Adjuster | Lender-Paid MI; rate is higher but no monthly MI to borrower. |
| 8 | Loan Amount Adjuster | Hit for very small loan balances (< $200k common threshold). |
| 9 | Minimum Loan Amount | Smallest loan the lender will price/fund. |
| 10 | Maximum Loan Amount | Largest loan eligible at that product/grid. |
| 11 | Property Inspection Waiver (PIW) Credit | Some lenders offer a small credit when an appraisal waiver is granted by DU/LP. |
| 12 | Appraisal Waiver / ACE / Value Acceptance | DU's PIW; Freddie's "Value Acceptance." |
| 13 | Temporary Buydown Adjuster | Hit for 2-1, 3-2-1, or 1-0 buydowns funded by seller/lender. |
| 14 | Float-Down Adjuster | Cost to enable a one-time float-down within an active lock. |
| 15 | Extended Lock Adjuster | Hit for 75/90/120-day locks (longer = more hedge cost). |
| 16 | Relock Penalty | Hit applied when a lock is broken and the loan is relocked. |
| 17 | Price Cap | Maximum premium price the lender will pay (e.g., capped at 103.000). |
| 18 | Price Floor | Minimum price the lender will offer at any rate. |
| 19 | SRP Cap | Maximum service-release premium reflected in price. |
| 20 | Pricing Tolerance | Allowed variance between locked and funded price before reprice/correction. |
| 21 | Stacking Rules | Order in which adjusters are applied; some lenders cap or net specific stacks. |
