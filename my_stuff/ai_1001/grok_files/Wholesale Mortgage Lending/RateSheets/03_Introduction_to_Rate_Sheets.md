# 03 — Introduction to Rate Sheets

**Prerequisite:** 02 — Wholesale Channel Basics

**Goal:** Understand what a rate sheet *is*, why wholesale lenders publish them daily (or intraday), and the basic logic of price-vs-rate.

## Concepts covered
- The purpose of a rate sheet in the wholesale channel
- Daily issuance, intraday reprices, and effective times
- The fundamental tradeoff: higher rate = higher price (rebate); lower rate = lower price (discount)
- Par, premium, and discount pricing
- How the broker reads a sheet to quote the borrower

## Jargon

| # | Term | Definition |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | Rate Sheet | A daily (or intraday) document/feed published by a wholesale lender showing available rates and the price the lender will pay (or charge) the broker for each rate at each product/term. |
| 2 | Daily Rate Sheet | Standard morning release; valid until reprice or end of day. |
| 3 | Reprice / Intraday Reprice | A mid-day republished sheet when market conditions move materially. |
| 4 | Effective Time | Timestamp the sheet becomes valid (e.g., 9:30 AM ET). |
| 5 | Expiration / Cut-Off | Time the sheet expires (often EOD or upon reprice). |
| 6 | Price | The dollar amount (per $100 of loan) the lender pays/charges for a given rate; expressed as a percentage of par. |
| 7 | Par (100.000) | Price at which neither rebate nor discount is owed. |
| 8 | Premium Pricing (>100) | Lender pays the broker a rebate (used to fund borrower costs or comp). |
| 9 | Discount Pricing (<100) | Borrower (or broker) pays points to obtain that rate. |
| 10 | Rebate / YSP (Yield Spread Premium) | Historic term for premium credit paid to the broker; today expressed as lender credit or broker comp. |
| 11 | Discount Points | Points paid up front to buy down the rate. |
| 12 | Rate/Price Grid | The matrix of rates (rows) vs lock periods (columns) showing prices. |
| 13 | Note Rate | The interest rate offered on the loan. |
| 14 | Coupon | The rate that is delivered into the security pool — drives investor pricing. |
| 15 | Pricing Tier | Grouping of borrowers/loans that share the same base price (e.g., 760+ FICO, 60 LTV tier). |
| 16 | Buy-Up / Buy-Down | Trading price for rate: buy-up takes a higher rate for more rebate; buy-down pays points for a lower rate. |
| 17 | All-In Price | Base price after all adjusters are applied. |
| 18 | Pricing Disclaimer | Lender's fine-print on lock policy, eligibility, and reprice rights. |
