# 02 — Mortgage Servicing

Covers the largest U.S. mortgage servicers (by UPB — unpaid principal balance) plus sub-servicers and specialty servicers. Servicing is the back half of the loan lifecycle: collecting payments, managing escrow, handling delinquency, transferring information to investors.

> Volumes are approximate; sourced from public 10-Q/10-K filings, Inside Mortgage Finance, and Mortgage Bankers Association reports. As of late 2024 / early 2025.

---

## What "Servicing" Actually Means

A servicer performs all post-funding administrative work on a loan:

| Function | What's involved |
|---|---|
| Payment processing | Collect P&I + escrow; remit to investor |
| Escrow management | Pay property tax + insurance from escrow |
| Investor reporting | Monthly remittance, performance data to GSE/investor |
| Customer service | Statements, payoff quotes, payment changes |
| Default management | Collections, loss mitigation, foreclosure |
| Loss mitigation | Modifications, short sales, deed-in-lieu |
| Disposition | REO marketing and sale |

Servicers earn ~25 basis points/year on UPB (servicing fee strip), plus float on escrow balances, plus ancillary fees.

### Sub-Servicer vs Servicer Distinction
| Role | Owns the MSR? | Earns servicing fee? | Capital risk? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Servicer | Yes | Yes | Yes (MSR value moves with rates) |
| Sub-servicer | No | No (per-loan fee instead) | Minimal |

## Industry Structure: Why Servicing Concentrated

The top 10 servicers control approximately 60% of all U.S. residential mortgage servicing UPB (~$8.5T total industry).

Drivers of concentration:
1. **Scale economies** — fixed costs (call centers, compliance, technology) amortize across larger UPBs.
2. **MSR market consolidation** — large MSR books trade between a small set of buyers.
3. **Regulatory burden** — CFPB and state servicing regs favor larger, well-capitalized servicers.
4. **Bank exits** — Wells Fargo, BofA, and others have sold off MSR portfolios; non-bank servicers (Mr. Cooper, Pennymac, Rithm) have been the buyers.

## Notable Operational Facts

- **Mr. Cooper** rebranded from **Nationstar Mortgage** in 2017 — explicitly to soften consumer perception (servicing was historically a "complaint magnet").
- **Cenlar FSB** is a federally chartered savings bank operating as a **mutual** — its sub-servicing customers are also (effectively) its owners. Unique structure in mortgage.
- **Sagent** was carved out of Mr. Cooper's technology arm in 2018 as a separate company backed by Warburg Pincus + Fiserv. Spun out to commercialize Mr. Cooper's servicing tech to the broader market.
- The **CFPB** has been particularly active in servicing — TILA-RESPA, periodic statements, error-resolution requirements, and dual-tracking prohibitions are major compliance drivers.
- A servicer must be approved by **Fannie**, **Freddie**, and **Ginnie** to service agency loans — these approvals require minimum net worth, operational standards, and ongoing review.

## Servicing vs Origination: Different Businesses

| Dimension | Origination | Servicing |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue type | Transaction (one-time) | Recurring (per-loan fee) |
| Volatility | Highly cyclical with rates | Stable; MSR value cyclical |
| People-intensity | Sales-led | Operations-led |
| Tech | LOS, PPE, AUS | Servicing platform, default tools |
| Regulatory primary | TRID, Reg Z, ATR | CFPB servicing rules, state regs |
| Profitability | Boom/bust | Steady fee income |
| Best run by | Sales-led firms | Operations-led firms |

The economic counterbalance: when rates rise (originations fall), MSR values rise (servicing book worth more) and vice versa. Multi-channel firms like Pennymac use this as a natural hedge.
