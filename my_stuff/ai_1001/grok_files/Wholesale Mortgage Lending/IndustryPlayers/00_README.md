## Why these three players matter

- **Optimal Blue** is the dominant U.S. mortgage product-and-pricing engine. Most loans are priced through it at some point.
- **ICE Mortgage Technology** (post-Black Knight) is now the dominant *infrastructure* layer — Encompass LOS, MSP servicing system, MERS, eRecording — through which a huge share of U.S. mortgages flow from origination to servicing.
- The **industry map** explains where every other meaningful company sits in relation to these two.

## A note on currency

Information here is current to early 2026. The mortgage-tech industry consolidates rapidly via M&A; expect ownership changes annually. The chronology is reliable; "who owns whom right now" is the part most likely to be stale.
