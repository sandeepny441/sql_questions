# HMDA action-taken breakdown — five UWM competitors (2018–2025)

The five competitors are the same 2025 originated-volume comparison set used in the lender analysis: Rocket Mortgage, CrossCountry Mortgage, PennyMac, JPMorgan Chase, and loanDepot. The public files support reliable LEI matching from 2018 onward; pre-2018 files use different respondent identifiers and are not included without a verified crosswalk.

Action-code meanings follow the FFIEC HMDA Guide (https://www.ffiec.gov/sites/default/files/data/hmda/2024Guide.pdf). “Total” is the sum of action codes 1–8. Purchased (6) is separate acquisition/repurchase activity, not additional originations.
