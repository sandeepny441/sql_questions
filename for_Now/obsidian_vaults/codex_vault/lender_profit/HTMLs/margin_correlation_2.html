Slope = −1.00: with the three constants held, each basis point added to margin is a basis point removed from the borrower. The dot is where we're pricing now; the breadcrumbs are where the piston has been.

Leadership read: choosing the margin is choosing the rate sheet. Widen it and the same borrower crosses par into points due; cut it and the credit grows — the market value of the loan never moved.
